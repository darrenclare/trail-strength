Trail Strength

15–20 minute home sessions for arm pump, leg pump and a stronger lower back. Rotate A → B → C, three times a week.

Choosing weight. Pick a dumbbell where the last 5 seconds are a fight but your form still holds. When it stops feeling like that, go up. Foundation weeks: go light and learn the movement.
Before your first session, watch one form video each for the goblet squat, single-leg RDL and bent-over row. The figure shows the movement, not fine form. These three hinge and squat movements are where your lower back is most at risk.
Scheduling. Not Session A the night before a big ride. Stop any exercise that causes sharp pain, or pain down a leg — that's a physio question, not a training one.
